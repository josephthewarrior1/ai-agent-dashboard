# Hermes HQ design direction

The office is the product: a tiny, inhabited workplace for the five existing Hermes bots. The selected character opens a compact inspector; the rest of the space remains usable.

Palette: porcelain #F4F5EF, chalk #FFFFFF, graphite #303A36, oak #C8A17E, sage #A9C4AA, sky #A6C9DF. Soft clay accents distinguish individual characters without a sci-fi interface.

Type: restrained Trebuchet MS display lettering; Segoe UI for controls and chat; Consolas only for tool output. System fonts keep the office self-contained.

Layout: a slim command rail and filters sit above one full-height interactive diorama. Camera controls and an activity ribbon sit on its edges. Details and conversations float over the scene, rather than permanently reducing it to a dashboard card.

Signature: a rounded architectural model, with warm oak, cutaway glass rooms and five tiny colleagues who physically travel between rooms. The chosen character carries its name and recent activity through the office.

The supplied brief determines the bright neutral palette. Interface decoration stays quiet so the office itself provides the identity. Area furniture communicates function without large area labels. Live source data and explicit simulation remain distinct.
